# Campus Drop — Brand Guidelines & Visual Identity

## 1. Brand Mission & Values

**Campus Drop** is built to provide an intuitive, secure, and modern peer-to-peer file-sharing experience. It stands as an entirely independent brand with a distinctive visual personality.

- **Speed**: Instant discovery without onboarding friction.
- **Privacy**: End-to-end encrypted direct data paths.
- **Accessibility**: Clear contrast ratios (WCAG AAA compliant), legible typography, and intuitive device node representations.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Color Palette

### Primary Color Tokens
- **Brand Primary (Indigo/Electric Blue)**:
  - HEX: `#3B82F6` (Blue 500) / `#2563EB` (Blue 600)
  - Purpose: Primary action buttons, active device rings, transfer progress bars.
- **Accent (Cyan/Aqua)**:
  - HEX: `#06B6D4` (Cyan 500)
  - Purpose: Radar discovery pulses, highlight accents.
- **Dark Mode Surface**:
  - Background: `#090D16` (Deep Midnight Blue)
  - Card / Dialog: `#111827` (Gray 900)
  - Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- **Light Mode Surface**:
  - Background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Card / Dialog: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)

---

## 3. Typography

- **Primary Font Family**: Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif.
- **Code & Pairing Keys**: Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `ui-monospace`).
  - Styled with letter-spacing `0.25em` for instant readability during cross-device pairing.

---

## 4. Logo & Iconography

- **Campus Drop Logo**: Dual concentric signal arcs descending toward a focal device node, symbolizing fluid, wireless campus peer sharing.
- **Device Node Icons**: Distinct icons for Mobile (`smartphone`), Desktop (`monitor`), Tablet (`tablet`), and Laptop (`laptop`).
